Why We Started

Bridging the gap between unreliable freelancers and expensive corporate software houses.

In Pakistan, over 80% of small business owners rely entirely on Facebook and Instagram pages to make sales. But when ad accounts get suspended or algorithm reach collapses, business grinds to a halt. Meanwhile, hiring a traditional agency often results in endless delays, five-figure upfront bills, and confusing technical jargon.

Digital Ustad was founded in 2023 in Lahore to solve exactly that. We created a practical, transparent model tailored for Pakistani realities: fast 7–10 day delivery, mobile-first design for 360px smartphones, seamless WhatsApp lead generation, and milestone-backed 25/50/25 installment plans.

Today, with over 10 live delivered websites and 5 ongoing retainer partnerships across retail, dining, healthcare, and services, we continue to help Pakistani entrepreneurs own their digital future with confidence.
