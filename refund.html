Refund & Cancellation Policy

Fair Milestone Protection • Clear Terms • Lahore, Pakistan

1. Principles of Fair Business

At Digital Ustad, our goal is 100% client satisfaction. We provide custom creative, engineering, and marketing services rather than pre-boxed commodities. Because our team commits dedicated developer and designer hours immediately upon agreement, this policy outlines the conditions governing refunds and project cancellations.

2. Project Milestone Refund Rules

A. Kickoff Deposit (25%)

The initial 25% kickoff payment covers initial design research, sitemap planning, and custom UI wireframing. If you request cancellation within 24 hours of payment and before design work begins, 100% of the deposit will be refunded (minus standard banking/EasyPaisa gateway fees). Once custom design work has commenced, the kickoff payment is non-refundable.

B. Approval Milestone (50%)

The 50% milestone payment is due only after you inspect the completed project on our live staging server and approve the design and functionality. Because this payment occurs after your formal inspection, it is non-refundable.

C. Final Balance (25%)

Due within 30 days post-launch. In the unlikely event of an unresolved technical defect covered under your package warranty, our team will resolve it promptly prior to final balance settlement.

D. 100% Upfront Packages (3-Page Lite & Small Projects)

For single-batch projects paid 100% upfront (such as 3-Page Lite), cancellations within 24 hours of payment and before design work begins are 100% refundable (less payment gateway fees). Once custom layout assembly has commenced, payments are non-refundable.

3. Monthly Retainer Cancellations

For ongoing monthly services (Social Media Management, SEO & Google Business, Meta & Google Ads, Graphic Design Retainers):

  • Retainers operate on a monthly advance cycle without lock-in contracts.
  • You may cancel or pause your retainer at any time by giving written notice via email or official contact channels at least 7 days prior to your next monthly billing date (Social Media retainers allow 7–15 days notice flexibility).
  • For organic SEO campaigns, while contracts remain flexible on a monthly basis, a 3-month continuous duration is recommended because search engine indexing requires progressive authority accumulation.
  • Partial-month refunds are not issued once active ad management, content creation, or SEO link-building has commenced for the active cycle.

4. Revision Policy & Commitment

We do not leave projects incomplete. Every website and creative package includes dedicated revision cycles. If an initial draft does not match your vision, we will revise the layouts, colors, typography, and content within the agreed package parameters to ensure you receive a product you are proud of.
