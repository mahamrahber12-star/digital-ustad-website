Payment & Hosting Transparency Policy

Clear guidelines so expectations are crystal clear before kickoff.

Hosting & Website Care Policy

Digital Ustad hosts and manages client websites on ultra-fast cloud servers. Website Care (PKR 3,000/mo) covers uptime monitoring, SSL certificates, weekly automated backups, and 2 minor monthly edits.

"Digital Ustad hosts and manages your website as part of Website Care. Hosting remains active while your account is in good standing. In the event of an outstanding balance beyond the agreed window, website availability may be paused until the account is resolved."
Ad Spend Transparency Rule

Our pricing quotes are management fees only. Actual ad spend is paid directly by you to Meta (Facebook/Instagram) or Google via your own debit/credit card. We never run ads from our card, ensuring 100% financial transparency and direct access to your analytics.

Domain Names & WhatsApp Meta Platform Fees

Domain Registration: Domain names (.com, .pk, etc.) are 100% owned by the client and purchased separately at direct registrar cost unless explicitly included in a custom proposal.
WhatsApp Cloud API Fees: Official Meta per-conversation message charges are billed directly by Meta to the credit card linked in your Meta Business Manager. Digital Ustad charges only for bot architecture and optional cloud webhook maintenance (PKR 3,000–5,000/mo).

Retainer Cancellation & No Lock-In Policy

Ongoing monthly retainers (Social Media, SEO, Paid Ads) operate on an advance monthly billing cycle with zero lock-in contracts. You may cancel or pause your retainer at any time by giving written notice at least 7 days prior to your next billing cycle. (For SEO campaigns, a 3-month compounding timeframe is recommended for organic indexing results).

Tax Policy Disclosure

All prices displayed across the website represent net professional agency fees in PKR. Provincial Sales Tax on Services (PRA / SRB / WHT) applicability is governed by official tax registration status and formal invoice issuance (Pending final business owner tax classification confirmation).
