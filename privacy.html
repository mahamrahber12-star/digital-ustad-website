LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026 • Digital Ustad, Lahore, Pakistan

1. Introduction & Overview

At Digital Ustad ("we", "our", or "us"), we value the trust placed in us by Pakistani small-and-medium businesses, entrepreneurs, and international clients. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is utilized, and how we ensure the utmost confidentiality of your business data, WhatsApp communications, and digital credentials.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to deliver high-quality digital agency services:

  • Contact & Business Identification: Name, WhatsApp phone number, business email, city of operation (e.g. Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad), and business vertical.
  • Project Specifications: Brand guidelines, design preferences, website reference links, menu files, inventory listings, and text copy provided for website creation.
  • Technical Access Credentials: Hosting server logins, domain registrar DNS credentials, Meta Business Manager access, or Google Search Console permissions required to deploy your website or manage ad campaigns.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for commercial and technical execution, including:

  • Preparing proposals, milestone scopes, and quotation estimates.
  • Direct communication via WhatsApp, telephone, or email regarding project revisions and delivery milestones.
  • Deploying websites to Netlify/servers, configuring DNS records, and setting up Google Business Profiles.
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your phone number, customer databases, or business assets to third-party telemarketers or advertisers.

4. Credential Security & Handover Protocol

We take credential security seriously. Any temporary hosting or domain passwords shared with our engineering team are stored in encrypted vaults during development and deleted upon project completion and final payment handover. We urge clients to reset access passwords once our handover documentation is provided.

5. Third-Party Services & Integrations

Depending on your service package, your project may integrate third-party infrastructure such as Meta Cloud API, WhatsApp Business API, Google Cloud / Search Console, or Netlify hosting. These providers maintain their own privacy standards and data policies.
