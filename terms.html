2. Standard Payment Policy & Milestone Structure

We operate with transparent payment milestones to protect both our engineering team and the client:

Option A: Full Upfront Payment

100% Upfront with 5% Discount

Applies automatically to returning clients or single-batch projects under PKR 25,000 (such as the 3-Page Lite package). Work begins immediately upon confirmation.

Option B: Website Flex Installment Plan (25 / 50 / 25)

Eligible Website Milestone Installments

Available specifically for eligible multi-page website packages (5-Page Starter and 7-Page Growth):

  • • 25% Kickoff: Mobilizes design, architecture & wireframes.
  • • 50% Approval: Due when website build is complete & approved on staging, prior to live DNS launch.
  • • 25% Final: Due within 30 days of live project delivery.
Precedence of Service-Specific Milestone Schedules

Service-Specific Agreements Govern

Where a specific proposal, package scope, service page, or written invoice specifies particular milestone terms, those service-specific terms explicitly take precedence over the general website flex schedule:

  • • Lite Packages & Sub-PKR 25,000 Projects: 100% advance upfront prior to kickoff (5% early-payment discount applies).
  • • Website Flex Packages (Starter & Growth): 25% kickoff, 50% staging approval, 25% within 30 days of live launch.
  • • Custom Web Applications, Portals & E-Commerce: 25% kickoff, 50% staging demo approval, 25% upon final live deployment/launch.
  • • WhatsApp Automation: Basic Bot is 50% kickoff / 50% live signoff & handover. Growth Workflow and Custom AI systems are 25% kickoff / 50% staging demo / 25% live deployment.
  • • Monthly Retainers (Social Media, SEO, Paid Ads, All-in-One): 100% advance monthly billing at the start of each service cycle. Paid ad spend is billed directly to the client's own payment card.
  • • Per-Project Graphic Design & Brand Identity: 50% kickoff deposit, 50% upon final creative delivery.

Important Notes: Milestone payments must be honored on schedule. Final code files, hosting credentials, and ownership rights transfer upon completion of the 50% approval milestone. Monthly retainers (Social Media, SEO, Paid Ads) are advance monthly billing only.

4. Delivery Timelines & Revisions

  • 3-Page Lite Website: 5–7 working days from receipt of brand assets.

5. Post-Launch Support Warranty & Website Care

Each website package includes our post-launch warranty:

  • 3-Page Lite: 7 days launch technical bugfix support.
  • 5-Page Starter: 14 days handover support & minor adjustments.
  • 7-Page Growth: 30 days full technical support included.
  • Website Care & Managed Hosting: Cloud hosting, 99.9% uptime monitoring, weekly backups, and minor edits are available via our Website Care Retainer at PKR 3,000/month. For Flex installment plans (Starter and Growth), Website Care is mandatory during the active balance window (until 30-day final balance settlement) to ensure staging stability, and optional thereafter.

6. Ad Spend, Domain Names & Third-Party Costs

Digital Ustad maintains strict financial transparency for all third-party expenses:

  • Media Ad Spend: For Meta and Google Ads services, our fees represent management retainers only. All actual advertising budget is billed directly to the client's debit/credit card via their own ad account.
  • Domain Registration: Domain names (.com, .pk) remain the 100% property of the client and are registered separately at registrar cost unless explicitly bundled in a custom proposal.
  • WhatsApp API Conversation Charges: Official Meta per-conversation message fees for WhatsApp Automation are billed directly by Meta via the client's Meta Business Manager payment card.

7. Monthly Retainer Terms & Cancellation Notice

Ongoing retainers (Social Media Management, SEO & Google Business, Paid Ads Management, and Design Retainers) operate on an advance monthly billing cycle with zero lock-in contracts. You may cancel or pause your retainer at any time by providing written notice at least 7 days prior to your next monthly billing date. For organic SEO campaigns, a 3-month compounding timeframe is recommended because search engine indexing requires progressive authority accumulation.
